# Product

## Register

product

## Users

School administrators, teachers, students, and parents use EduTrack during the school day and at home. Admins manage classes, staff, students, and subjects. Teachers mark attendance, create assignments, and grade work. Students submit assignments and check grades. Parents monitor their children's attendance and performance. Context is task-heavy: forms, tables, dashboards, and role-specific workflows.

## Product Purpose

EduTrack is a comprehensive education management platform that centralizes school operations: roster management, attendance, assignments, grading, and role-based dashboards. Success means staff complete daily tasks quickly with clear data, and families trust what they see without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and calm. Voice is direct and instructional, not promotional. The interface should feel like dependable school infrastructure: familiar patterns, readable data, and confidence without flash.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing-page clichés (hero metrics, gradient text, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids). Dark-mode purple gradients and neon accents. Overly playful consumer apps that undermine institutional trust. Marketing buzzword copy ("streamline," "empower," "seamless"). AI-default warm cream backgrounds and decorative motion for its own sake.

## Design Principles

1. **Task first.** Every screen serves a job; decoration never competes with data or actions.
2. **Earned familiarity.** Use standard product patterns (tables, forms, nav) so users fluent in modern tools trust the UI immediately.
3. **Role clarity.** Navigation and permissions match who is signed in; never blur admin vs teacher vs parent contexts.
4. **Readable at a glance.** Hierarchy, contrast, and spacing make counts, statuses, and deadlines scannable under normal office lighting.
5. **Honest states.** Loading, empty, error, and success states teach what to do next; never leave a blank or ambiguous panel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for body text and controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Ensure focus rings remain visible on keyboard navigation. Form errors must be programmatically associated and described in plain language.
